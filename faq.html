SERVING SARGODHA

Frequently asked questions

The Fix Crew currently provides home services in Sargodha only. Here are answers to common questions about booking and service.

01How do I book a technician with The Fix Crew?+

Choose whichever booking method works best for you:

  • Website: Submit the appointment form, then review and send the prefilled WhatsApp message.
  • Email: Send questions to thefixcrewofficial@gmail.com.
  • WhatsApp: Message us at 0309 7862739 with the service you need and your location.
  • Phone: Call our helpline to speak with a representative and arrange a suitable appointment time.
02What can I expect when I book with The Fix Crew?+

Our service is built around dependable technicians and clear communication. You can expect:

  • Skilled, polite technicians who have completed background checks.
  • Arrival within the time window confirmed for your booking.
  • Clear communication throughout your service.
  • Customer support and follow-up after the service.
03How much is the inspection visit?+

Please contact us for a quote for the on-site inspection and diagnosis. If you approve the estimate and proceed with the repair, the inspection fee is adjusted against your final bill.

04What hours can I book a service?+

Our technicians and customer support are available 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM, seven days a week, including Saturdays and Sundays. We prioritize emergency repair requests for dispatch.

05Which locations does The Fix Crew cover?+

We currently serve Sargodha only. Within the city, our service area includes locations such as Satellite Town, Cantt, University Road, Fatima Jinnah Colony, and the PAF Base vicinity. We do not currently provide services in other cities.

06Can I change or cancel my appointment?+

Yes. You can reschedule or cancel at no charge any time before the technician has left for your address.

07What should I do if a repair needs follow-up?+

If something is wrong with a completed job, email thefixcrewofficial@gmail.com. Our support team will review your concern and arrange a priority follow-up.

08Can I provide my own replacement parts?+

Parts and materials are charged separately from labor. You can either:

  • Provide the parts yourself, if you prefer.
  • Ask our technician to source genuine replacement parts from an authorized hardware supplier. The technician will provide the store receipt.
09How are technicians checked before a home visit?+

Before a technician is dispatched, we verify their identity and CNIC, assess their practical technical skills, and provide customer etiquette training.

10How can I pay after the work is done?+

After the service is complete and you are satisfied with the work, you can pay by:

  • Cash on Delivery (COD)
  • JazzCash
  • EasyPaisa
  • Direct Online Bank Transfer
