Frequently asked questions

Find answers about bookings, service visits, coverage, and the care we provide.

01How can I schedule an appointment with a professional technician from The Fix Crew?+

To book our professional services, you have three convenient options:

  • Online Booking: Submit a quick service request directly through our website booking form.
  • WhatsApp: Send us a message on our official WhatsApp number with your required service and location.
  • Direct Call: Call our helpline directly to speak with a representative and lock in an immediate time slot.
02What sets The Fix Crew apart as a trusted home maintenance provider?+

The Fix Crew is committed to eliminating the hassle of unvetted local handymen. We distinguish ourselves through:

  • Rigorously background-checked, skilled, and polite technicians.
  • Punctual arrival within guaranteed time windows.
  • Upfront, honest pricing with zero hidden charges.
  • Dedicated customer support and post-service follow-up.
03What are the inspection and visit charges?+

We charge a nominal inspection fee of Rs. 300 to Rs. 500 for on-site fault assessment and diagnosis. If you approve the estimate and proceed with the repair work with our technician, this inspection fee is adjusted directly into your final service bill.

04What are your service booking timings?+

Our service technicians and customer support desk operate from 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM, 7 days a week (including Saturdays and Sundays). Emergency repair requests are prioritized for swift dispatch.

05In which areas can I avail The Fix Crew services?+

Our services are currently active across all key residential and commercial sectors in Sargodha, including Satellite Town, Cantt, University Road, Fatima Jinnah Colony, PAF Base vicinity, and surrounding areas. We are actively expanding to neighboring cities soon.

06Can I cancel or reschedule my booking?+

Yes. You have complete flexibility to reschedule or cancel your service booking free of charge at any stage before the technician has departed for your premises.

07How do I register a complaint or request a rework?+

Customer satisfaction is our utmost priority. If you encounter any issue or are dissatisfied with a completed job, you can register a complaint via our website contact form, direct WhatsApp message, or helpline call. Our support team will review the case and arrange a priority follow-up.

08Does The Fix Crew offer any warranty or service guarantee?+

Yes. We back our labor with a 7-Day Check Warranty. If the exact same issue resurfaces within 7 days of the completed service, our technician will revisit and resolve the fault without any extra labor inspection fee.

09Who is responsible for purchasing spare parts or replacement materials?+

Material and spare parts costs are separate from labor charges:

  • You are welcome to supply your own parts if you prefer.
  • Alternatively, our technician can purchase genuine, high-quality replacement parts from authorized hardware suppliers on your behalf, accompanied by an authentic store receipt.
10Are your technicians safe and verified to enter my home?+

Safety and integrity are non-negotiable. Every technician operating under The Fix Crew undergoes thorough identity verification (CNIC verification), practical technical vetting, and customer etiquette training before being dispatched to your doorstep.

11What payment methods do you accept?+

Once the service is completed to your total satisfaction, you can pay via:

  • Cash on Delivery (COD)
  • JazzCash
  • EasyPaisa
  • Direct Online Bank Transfer
